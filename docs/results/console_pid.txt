selected architecture: pump
  - required coolant feed pressure ~61 bar exceeds 85% of LCH4's critical pressure (46 bar): a saturated-ullage cryo tank cannot be pressure-fed that high, and subcritical heated coolant would boil in the jacket -> electric pump on the coolant line, tank held at a low autogenous pad (2-4 bar)
  - autogenous tank pad is a natural upgrade here: the regen outlet already provides warm vapor of the same species (see the 'autogenous' scheme for that variant)
Feed system recommendation — pressurization: pump
==================================================================

[engine]
  PU-001   Electric feed pump (coolant side)
           - raises channel pressure above the propellant critical pressure (supercritical cooling, avoids two-phase flow in the jacket)
  PT-020   Coolant manifold inlet pressure transducer
           - confirms jacket inlet pressure (supercritical margin / injector stiffness)
  TC-021   Coolant outlet temperature sensor
           - the regen outlet state IS the injector inlet state — monitors heat pickup and coking/decomposition margin
  RV-022   Coolant-jacket relief path
           - protects trapped coolant volume between valves against heat-soak overpressure after shutdown
  PT-023   Chamber pressure transducer
           - primary performance and abort parameter
  IGN-024  Igniter (with its own isolation)
           - bi-propellant engines are not hypergolic — positive ignition source with independent control

[pressurant]
  QD-002   Low-pressure ullage pad charge port
           - pump-fed tanks still need a small ullage pad (1-4 bar) for pump NPSH and structural stability

[LOX]
  FDV-003  LOX fill/drain valve (cryo-rated)
           - ground fill and safe de-tanking path; cryo service needs cold-rated seats/seals
  VV-004   LOX remote vent valve
           - boil-off management during hold; venting is the primary abort/safe action for a cryogenic tank
  RV-005   LOX relief valve
           - primary overpressure protection sized for the worst-case heating (self-pressurization) rate
  BD-006   LOX burst disc
           - redundant, non-reclosing overpressure protection in parallel with the relief valve (standard practice for flight tanks)
  PT-007   LOX tank pressure transducer
           - ullage pressure: feed margin monitoring + abort criteria
  TC-008   LOX tank temperature sensors (2x)
           - bulk liquid + ullage temperature: fill level inference and stratification/boil-off monitoring
  FLT-009  LOX feed-line filter
           - protects injector orifices and valve seats from debris
  MV-010   LOX main valve (remote, fast-acting)
           - engine start/stop; abort isolation of the propellant supply
  PGV-011  LOX line purge check valve (inert gas)
           - pre-ignition inerting and post-fire clearing of the oxidizer line (fire safety)

[LCH4]
  FDV-012  LCH4 fill/drain valve (cryo-rated)
           - ground fill and safe de-tanking path; cryo service needs cold-rated seats/seals
  VV-013   LCH4 remote vent valve
           - boil-off management during hold; venting is the primary abort/safe action for a cryogenic tank
  RV-014   LCH4 relief valve
           - primary overpressure protection sized for the worst-case heating (self-pressurization) rate
  BD-015   LCH4 burst disc
           - redundant, non-reclosing overpressure protection in parallel with the relief valve (standard practice for flight tanks)
  PT-016   LCH4 tank pressure transducer
           - ullage pressure: feed margin monitoring + abort criteria
  TC-017   LCH4 tank temperature sensors (2x)
           - bulk liquid + ullage temperature: fill level inference and stratification/boil-off monitoring
  FLT-018  LCH4 feed-line filter
           - protects injector orifices and valve seats from debris
  MV-019   LCH4 main valve (remote, fast-acting)
           - engine start/stop; abort isolation of the propellant supply
